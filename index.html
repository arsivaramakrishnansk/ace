What size (tonnage) of AC do I need?

It depends on room area, sun exposure, floor level, number of people and heat from appliances. As a rough guide, many average rooms of 100–150 sq.ft need about 1 ton and 150–200 sq.ft about 1.5 tons in our climate. Try the AC size calculator, then let our engineer confirm the size with a free site visit.

Inverter or non-inverter: which should I buy?

For rooms used for several hours a day, an inverter AC usually costs less to run because the compressor slows down instead of switching on and off. It also holds the temperature more steadily and runs more quietly.

What do star ratings mean?

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) star label tells you how efficient an AC is: more stars means lower running cost for the same cooling. Star ratings for room ACs are based on ISEER (Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio). Read our star ratings explainer.

Cassette, ductable or VRV for my office or hall?

Cassettes suit open areas with a false ceiling. Ductable systems give balanced cooling across large halls with only grilles visible. VRV suits buildings with many rooms that each need their own control. We recommend after a site study. See our comparison guide.

How long does a split AC installation take?

A standard split AC installation is usually completed the same day once the site is ready. Larger ductable, VRV or central systems are planned as projects with a schedule we agree with you.

Do you do a site visit before installation?

Yes. Every project starts with a proper site study: room size, heat load, wall and ceiling structure, outdoor unit location, drainage and electrical supply.
