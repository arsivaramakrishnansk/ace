About us

Sixteen years of keeping the Delta cool.

Founded in Kumbakonam in 2010 by Mr. R. Saravanan, Air Control Engineers has grown from single split ACs to the Delta's first 140 TR project, without ever losing its service-first promise.

Who we are

An air conditioner is only as good as the service behind it.

Air Control Engineers is a Kumbakonam-based HVAC company and an authorised dealer for Daikin air conditioners.

For over 16 years, homes, hospitals, schools, showrooms and marriage halls in more than 20 cities across Tamil Nadu have relied on us to keep their spaces cool.

Every project, from a single split AC to a 140 TR central system, comes with a proper site study, neat and correct installation, and dependable after-sales service for the life of the system.

  • Authorised Daikin dealer: genuine products, spares and warranty support
  • Domestic, commercial, industrial and AMC under one roof
  • Trained engineers for split, cassette, ductable, VRV and AHU systems

From the founder's desk

We don't disappear after the installation.

When I started Air Control Engineers in 2010, I made one promise to every customer: we will stay with you long after the work is done. An air conditioner keeps a hospital safe, a showroom welcoming and a wedding hall comfortable, so when it needs attention, we are there.

Sixteen years on, that promise has carried us from single split ACs to the Delta's first 140 TR project, around 25 marriage halls in and around Kumbakonam, and every Kalyan Jewellers branch in the region.

Thank you for considering us. We look forward to keeping your spaces comfortable for years to come.

R. SaravananFounder & Managing Director, Air Control Engineers
