Know the signs

When does your AC need a service call?

Small symptoms become expensive failures. Call us if you notice any of these.

Report a problem

Weak cooling

Room takes much longer to cool, or never reaches the set temperature.

Rising power bills

Dirty coils and low gas make the compressor work harder for the same cooling.

Water leaking indoors

Usually a blocked drain line or incorrect slope; easy to fix early.

Noise or error codes

Rattles, clicking or blinking codes point to fan, PCB or sensor issues.
