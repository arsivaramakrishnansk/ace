Comprehensive vs non-comprehensive AMC: which one fits you?

26 Aug 20264 min readBy the ACE engineering team

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) turns AC care from "call someone when it breaks" into a plan. But not all AMCs cover the same things. Here is a clear comparison.

Non-comprehensive AMC

Covers scheduled preventive maintenance visits and labour for breakdown calls. Any spare parts needed, such as a capacitor, fan motor or PCB, are charged separately.

  • Lower annual fee.
  • Good for newer ACs that are still under manufacturer warranty, or units with a low risk of part failure.
  • You still get priority response and regular servicing.

Comprehensive AMC

Includes everything above, plus the cost of most spare parts, as listed in the contract. Some items, typically things like plastic body parts, remote controls or damage from external causes, are usually excluded, so always read the list.

  • Higher annual fee, but a predictable budget.
  • Good for older ACs, heavily used commercial units and sites where downtime is costly.
  • Removes the "should we repair or not?" debate when a part fails.

Who should choose what?

SituationWe usually suggest
New home ACs, light useNon-comprehensive
Home ACs out of warranty, heavy useComprehensive
Clinics, hospitals, jewellery showroomsComprehensive with priority response
Brands with several branchesMulti-site AMC with one point of contact

Five questions to ask before you sign

  1. How many preventive visits per year, and in which months?
  2. What is the response time for breakdowns?
  3. Exactly which parts are covered, and which are excluded?
  4. Are genuine manufacturer spares used?
  5. Is gas charging included, and under what conditions?

We answer all five in writing before any AMC starts. Ask for a free AMC quote for your home or business.
