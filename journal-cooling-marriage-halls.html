Cooling a marriage hall: what 25+ mahals have taught us

08 Aug 20266 min readBy the ACE engineering team

Kumbakonam is a city of weddings, and we have air-conditioned around 25 marriage halls in and around it, including the Delta's first 140 TR project at KRG Pavalam Mahal. Mahals are a unique engineering challenge.

Why mahals are different

  • Extreme peaks. A hall can go from empty to packed in an hour. Each guest adds body heat and moisture, so the cooling load at the muhurtham can be several times the load of an empty hall.
  • Heat from every side. Stage lighting, decorative lights, generators, and kitchens and dining halls nearby all add load.
  • Big volumes, high ceilings. Hot air rises; the system must deliver cool air where guests actually sit.
  • Intermittent use. Halls may run hard on wedding days and sit idle between bookings, which affects how the plant should be sized and staged.

How we approach a hall

  1. Heat-load study for the peak, not the average. We size for a full house on a hot afternoon, then design the system so it can run efficiently at part load too.
  2. Choose the right system. Ductable units and AHUs with well-designed ducting give even cooling and keep the interiors clean. Floor-standing units can supplement specific zones like the stage or dining area.
  3. Stage the capacity. Multiple units or compressors let the hall manager bring cooling online in steps, pre-cooling before guests arrive instead of fighting the heat once they do.
  4. Treat the dining hall and kitchen separately. Exhaust and ventilation keep kitchen heat and smells out of the main hall.
  5. Plan for service. Access panels, isolation valves and clear plant areas make maintenance quick, because nobody wants a breakdown on a wedding day.
Tip for hall owners: run the system for an hour or so before guests arrive. It is far easier to hold a hall at 24 °C than to pull it down from 34 °C with 800 people inside.

After the handover

For halls, we strongly recommend a comprehensive AMC with a pre-season service before the main wedding months and priority breakdown response. It is the same service-first promise we make to every client: we don't disappear after the installation.

Planning a new hall or upgrading an old one? Talk to our projects team.
