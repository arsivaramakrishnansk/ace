Privacy note.

How we handle the details you share with us.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics by default. When you use the service-request form, your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device; nothing is stored on this website. If you call, WhatsApp or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, address and AC details.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry, schedule visits, provide quotes, deliver AMC services and keep service records for your equipment. We do not sell or rent your information.

Third-party services

The contact page embeds Google Maps and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts; these services may process technical data such as your IP address under their own policies. WhatsApp messages are handled under WhatsApp's terms.

Your choices
